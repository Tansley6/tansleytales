Benny and the Secret Meadow

A Tansley Tales StoryCreated and read by Tania JacobsWords and pictures made with AI, then edited and checked by Tania

Story timeKindness and courage4 min read

Benny the bunny, Max the mouse and Lila the ladybird set off to find a secret meadow. A wobbly log, prickly bushes and a very grumpy badger stand in their way.

The next morning, Benny the bunny hopped out into the warm, golden sunshine.

He packed five juicy strawberries for lunch.

He was ready for a new adventure!

Lila the ladybird landed on a daisy beside him.

Max the Mouse came scurrying up with a crumpled map.

“Look!” said Max. “It shows the way to the Secret Meadow!”

“The Secret Meadow is full of flowers and sweet, ripe berries,” said Lila.

“But a big, grumpy badger lives right by the gate,” squeaked Max.

Benny’s tummy did a little flip.

Then Benny remembered the stream, the dark cave and the lily pads.

“Being brave means trying, even when you feel afraid,” he said.

“We’ll face it together!” said Lila.

The path led to a fast, rushing stream.

The only way across was a wobbly old log.

It was much bigger than the stream Benny had crossed before.

Benny took a deep breath.

Hop. Wobble. Hop. Wobble. Hop!

He made it! Max scampered across behind him, and Lila fluttered over the top.

Next, a wall of thick, prickly bushes blocked their way.

Lila flew up high to look.

“There’s a gap!” she called. “Follow me!”

Max sniffed the ground with his clever nose.

“Mind the sticky mud,” he said. “Step on the stones.”

Together, they squeezed through the gap. Not one of them got stuck.

On the other side was a wooden gate, covered in flowers.

Beside it was a big hole under an old oak tree.

Suddenly, out popped a HUGE, stripy badger.

“Who’s making all that noise?” grumbled the badger. “I was fast asleep!”

Max hid behind Benny. Benny’s knees went wibble-wobble.

But the badger didn’t look scary now. He looked tired.

Benny stepped forward.

“I’m sorry we woke you up,” he said. “Would you like a strawberry?”

He held out the biggest, juiciest strawberry from his lunch.

The badger blinked. Then a smile spread across his stripy face.

“Nobody has ever said sorry to me before,” he said. “They usually run away.”

“I’m Barnaby. Would you like to see the meadow?”

Barnaby opened the gate.

The Secret Meadow was even more beautiful than the map!

There were flowers of every colour, and berries everywhere they looked.

They had a picnic in the long grass.

Benny shared his last four strawberries, and Barnaby shared the sweetest blackberries in the meadow.

Bees buzzed, butterflies danced, and everyone giggled until the sun went down.

“Goodnight, Barnaby!” said Benny with a yawn.

“Good morning!” laughed Barnaby. Badgers wake up when the stars come out.

That night, Benny snuggled into bed.

Being brave had taken him all the way to the gate.

But being kind had opened it.

The End
