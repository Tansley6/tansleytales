The Lion Who Was Afraid of the Dark

A Tansley Tales StoryCreated and read by Tania JacobsWords and pictures made with AI, then edited and checked by Tania

Bedtime storyFaith6 min read

Lumo is the biggest and strongest lion on the grasslands, but he has a secret: he is afraid of the dark. One night a tiny firefly helps him find his way home. A Faith Story about knowing that God is always with you.

Reading style

Long ago, when animals could talk, there lived a great lion named Lumo.

Lumo was the biggest and strongest animal on the grasslands.

His paws were enormous.

His teeth were sharp.

And his roar made the trees tremble.

ROOOAAAR!

“Lumo is afraid of nothing!” the animals would say.

Lumo always smiled when he heard this.

But the animals were wrong.

Lumo was afraid of the dark.

Every evening, before the sun disappeared, Lumo hurried back to his cave.

He never joined the other animals beneath the stars.

“I need my rest,” he would say.

But Lumo did not sleep.

He stayed awake, listening to every sound outside his cave.

One afternoon, Lumo walked farther than usual while searching for water.

He crossed a dry riverbed.

He climbed over a rocky hill.

At last, he found a clear pool.

Lumo drank until he was no longer thirsty.

When he lifted his head, the sun had disappeared.

The grasslands were dark.

Lumo tried to remember the way home, but every rock looked the same.

A branch snapped behind him.

CRACK!

“Who is there?” Lumo roared.

A tiny firefly appeared beside his nose.

“It is only me,” she said.

“I knew that,” Lumo replied quickly. “I was not frightened.”

The firefly flew around Lumo’s shaking paws.

“You may be able to fool the other animals,” she said kindly, “but you cannot fool me.”

Lumo lowered his head.

“I am afraid of the dark,” he admitted. “Please do not tell anyone.”

“There is no shame in feeling afraid,” said Firefly. “Come. I will help you find your way home.”

“You?” asked Lumo. “But you are so small!”

“And you are very big,” Firefly replied. “Yet tonight, you need my light.”

Firefly flew ahead, and Lumo followed her tiny glow.

Soon, they reached two paths.

The wide path looked smooth and easy.

The narrow path wound between tall, dark rocks.

“We should take the wide path,” said Lumo.

“No,” said Firefly. “That path leads to the crocodiles’ river. The narrow path leads home.”

Lumo did not like the narrow path.

But he trusted Firefly.

She stayed close to him as they walked between the rocks.

Whenever Lumo took a wrong turn, she gently guided him back.

Whenever he heard a frightening sound, her little light reminded him that he was not alone.

At last, they reached the top of a hill.

Below them lay Lumo’s cave.

The other animals were waiting for him.

“Where have you been?” asked Elephant.

Lumo wanted to pretend that he had been on a great adventure.

Then he looked at Firefly.

“I became lost,” he admitted. “I was frightened of the dark, and Firefly stayed with me and showed me the way home.”

The animals stared in surprise.

Then Elephant stepped forward.

“I am afraid of bees,” he confessed.

“I am afraid of deep water,” said Cheetah.

“And I am afraid of storms,” said Buffalo.

No one laughed.

From that night on, Lumo stopped pretending to be fearless.

He still had enormous paws, sharp teeth and a mighty roar.

But now he understood that even the strongest animal sometimes needs help.

He also learned that a tiny light can guide even the greatest lion home.

The End
