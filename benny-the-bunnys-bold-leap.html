Benny the Bunny’s Bold Leap

A Tansley Tales StoryCreated and read by Tania JacobsWords and pictures made with AI, then edited and checked by Tania

Story timeCourage and friendship4 min read

Benny the bunny is very shy, and he wishes he could be as brave as the birds in the sky. With a little help from his friends, he finds out that being brave means trying, even when you feel afraid.

Reading style

Benny the bunny hid behind a big, green, leafy bush.

He was very shy.

He wanted to be as brave as the birds flying in the big blue sky.

He sighed a big, sad sigh.

Lila the ladybird landed on a leaf near Benny, smiling.

“What’s wrong, Benny?” she asked kindly.

Benny whispered, “I’m afraid of everything.”

Lila smiled. “Being brave doesn’t mean you’re never scared,” she said.

“It’s about trying, even if you feel afraid!”

Lila pointed to a gurgling stream.

“I’m afraid to cross the stream, but today I will try. Let’s cross!”

Benny hesitated.

He took a deep breath and hopped.

He made it! He couldn’t believe it.

It felt great! He’d been brave!

As they continued their journey, they met their friend, Max the Mouse.

They came across a colourful flower garden with tall sunflowers and bright daisies.

Benny felt nervous, but he remembered that being brave didn’t mean you weren’t nervous, and he had his friends by his side.

Lila and Max encouraged him to take small steps through the garden.

They worked together as a team!

They saw a dark cave ahead. It looked scary.

“I’m afraid,” whispered Benny.

Lila stayed close beside him, and they walked together slowly.

Soon, they saw the sunlight, and Benny knew the scary part was over!

As they continued their journey, they came across a small pond with lily pads scattered across its surface.

On the far side, they saw a group of friendly frogs having a party.

“Oh, it looks like so much fun over there!” Max exclaimed.

“But how can we join them?”

Benny felt nervous about crossing the water.

“I don’t know if I can do it,” he whispered.

Lila smiled encouragingly.

“Remember, we can do this together,” she said kindly.

“Let’s think of a way to get across!”

They looked around and spotted some large, flat leaves nearby.

Working as a team, they gathered the leaves and placed them on the water, creating a path of makeshift rafts.

“Now we can hop from leaf to leaf!” Max said excitedly.

Benny took a deep breath.

With Lila and Max cheering him on, he made the first hop onto a leaf.

Then another, and another.

His friends followed closely behind.

When they reached the other side, the frogs welcomed them with cheers.

Benny couldn’t believe he’d done it!

He felt proud and happy as they joined the lily pad party.

Everyone cheered! Benny had lost his shyness.

He realised that being brave didn’t mean never being afraid.

It meant doing your best, especially with friends by your side.

He knew he could do anything!

That night, Benny went to sleep, dreaming of his next adventure.

He knew he could do anything with Lila and Max by his side.

The End
