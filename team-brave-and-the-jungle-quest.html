Team Brave and the Jungle Quest

Emily, Riley and Reid call themselves Team Brave. When they dig up an old map in the garden, it leads them into a hidden jungle, where they will need every bit of teamwork to finish the quest. True jungle facts are at the end.

Chapter 1The Old Map

Emily, Riley and Reid were best friends.

They called themselves Team Brave.

They were always ready for an adventure.

One sunny morning, they were playing in Emily’s garden.

Poppy, Emily’s little dog, was digging in a pile of leaves.

Suddenly she barked.

Under the leaves was a crumpled old map.

The paper was yellow and torn at the edges.

Across the top, in curly letters, it said:

MAP TO THE MYSTIC MONKEY GEM.

Hidden deep in the jungle!

Riley’s eyes grew wide. “Is this real?”

Emily brushed off the dirt. “It looks real to me!”

Reid grinned. “Then let’s go on a jungle quest!”

They packed snacks, torches and two walkie-talkies.

Poppy wagged her tail so hard her whole body wiggled.

Emily’s gran was looking after all three of them that day.

She waved from the back door.

“Stay together,” she called. “And be back before dark!”

“We will!” said Team Brave.

Chapter 2The Tree with a Twist

The jungle was at the edge of town.

Past the playground.

Behind Mr Talbot’s farm.

Nobody in town ever went there.

Most people didn’t even know it was there.

The trees grew tall and thick.

The leaves were shiny and green.

Colourful butterflies floated by like tiny kites.

“This place is amazing!” said Reid.

Riley pointed up. “Look! A parrot with red, blue and yellow feathers!”

Emily opened the map.

“First we have to find the Tree with a Twist.”

They stepped over roots and ducked under vines.

Poppy sniffed every leaf and every log.

Emily kept her eyes on the map.

Then they saw it.

A giant tree with a trunk that twisted round and round like a corkscrew.

“The Tree with a Twist!” said Emily.

Chapter 3The Talking Parrot

The parrot flew down and landed on a branch above them.

“Squawk! Only the brave may pass!” it said.

“Answer me this. What has a face and two hands, but no arms or legs?”

Team Brave looked at each other.

“A face and hands,” said Riley slowly. “But no arms or legs?”

Emily thought hard.

“Is it a clock?”

The parrot flapped its wings.

“Correct! Squawk! Follow the purple flowers!”

A narrow path, lined with purple flowers, led deeper into the jungle.

“Thank you, Mr Parrot!” said Reid.

And off they marched.

Chapter 4The Bridge of Wobbles

Soon they came to a river.

The water rushed and roared far below.

Across it hung a long, narrow rope bridge.

Riley gulped. “That’s a long way down.”

“We’re Team Brave,” said Emily. “We can do this. One at a time.”

Reid went first.

He held a rope in each hand.

He took one careful step.

Then another.

Halfway across, the wind blew and the bridge wobbled.

Reid stood still and held on tight until it stopped.

Then he kept going, all the way to the other side.

Reid took out his walkie-talkie.

“It’s fine!” his voice crackled. “Hold on with both hands. Don’t look down!”

Riley went next.

His knees were shaking.

“You can do it, Riley!” Reid called from the other side.

“Nearly there!” Emily called from behind.

Step by step, Riley made it across.

Last came Emily.

Poppy was zipped into the big pocket of her backpack, with just her head poking out.

Emily held the ropes with both hands and walked slowly across.

Poppy didn’t make a sound.

“Phew!” said Reid. “We did it!”

They sat down and shared their snacks.

Poppy got a dog biscuit.

Chapter 5The Cave

Past the bridge, the jungle grew darker.

The trees blocked out the sun.

Behind a curtain of vines was the opening of a cave.

“The map says we go through here,” said Emily.

They switched on their torches.

The cave was cool and damp.

Bats fluttered high above.

The walls sparkled with crystals.

Old tree roots had grown down through the rock and twisted across the floor.

Then they heard a small, sad cry.

Poppy stopped and stared into the dark.

Riley shone his torch.

It was a jaguar cub.

Its back paw was stuck in a knot of roots.

“It’s only a baby,” whispered Riley.

“We mustn’t touch it,” said Emily. “It’s wild. But we can help.”

Emily and Riley shone their torches on the roots.

Reid found a long stick.

Very gently, he lifted the roots.

The cub pulled its paw free.

It gave a soft, happy chuff.

Then it padded deeper into the cave.

It stopped and looked back at them.

“I think it wants us to follow,” said Reid.

Chapter 6The Hidden Temple

The cave opened into a sunny clearing.

In the centre stood an old stone temple, covered in moss and vines.

“We found it!” whispered Reid.

The walls were carved with monkeys and jewels.

A big stone door was shut tight.

The jaguar cub jumped onto a stone step and pressed it with its paw.

RUMBLE.

Slowly, the door opened.

Inside, the air sparkled with dust.

In the middle of the room stood a stone stand, carved like a bunch of bananas.

On top sat a glowing red gem.

“The Mystic Monkey Gem!” gasped Emily.

Chapter 7The Last Challenge

Riley reached for the gem.

The ground shook.

A deep voice filled the room.

“Choose the one thing you could not have done without.”

Three stone monkeys on the wall moved their arms.

Behind them were three buttons.

One said Bravery.

One said Kindness.

One said Teamwork.

“Bravery,” said Riley. “We crossed the bridge.”

“But I only crossed because you two were cheering me on,” said Reid.

“Kindness,” said Emily. “We helped the cub.”

“But we needed all three of us to do it,” said Riley.

“Two torches and one stick.”

They looked at each other and smiled.

“Teamwork,” they said together.

They put their hands on the button.

And pressed.

The gem rose into the air.

It floated down, soft as a feather, into Emily’s hands.

“You have chosen well, Team Brave,” said the voice.

“The gem is a gift. It is yours to keep.

Look after it.

One day, it will call you back.”

Chapter 8Back Before Dark

The jungle seemed brighter on the way back.

The birds sang louder.

The leaves seemed to whisper, “Well done.”

The jaguar cub walked with them through the cave.

When they came out the other side, it stopped and sniffed the air.

There, in the shadows of the trees, stood a big jaguar.

The cub’s mum.

The cub ran to her.

She looked at the children for a long moment.

Then mother and cub slipped away into the jungle together.

“It found its mum,” said Riley softly.

Team Brave crossed the Bridge of Wobbles again.

One at a time.

Both hands on the ropes.

This time, nobody’s knees were shaking.

As they stepped out of the jungle, the gem’s glow slowly faded.

Now it looked just like an ordinary red stone.

They got back to Emily’s garden just as the sun went down.

Emily’s gran brought out sandwiches.

“Did you have a nice day?” she asked.

“The best,” said Emily.

Only Team Brave knew the secret.

They put the gem in a box.

On the lid they wrote:

Team Brave’s First Adventure.

That night, the first stars came out.

“Do you think there are more adventures out there?” asked Riley.

“I hope so,” said Emily.

“Because Team Brave is just getting started.”

Inside the box, very faintly, something began to glow.

The End
